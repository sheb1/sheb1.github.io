Privacy Policy

Islamic Inheritance Calculator (Faraid) · Last updated: 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Islamic Inheritance Calculator (Faraid) Android app ("the app") handles your information. In short: the app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The heir details and estate value you enter are used only on your device to perform the calculation and are not transmitted anywhere. They are not saved after you close the app unless you choose to export them.

Location (prayer times)

The Prayer times section can use your device's approximate location to work out Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for where you are. This is optional: you can pick a city from a list instead, and the app only asks for location when you tap "Use my location."

The location is used only on your device to calculate the times. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and the app has no network access to send it with. The app keeps your last location and your prayer-time settings in its private storage on the device so the times are ready next time; uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them. You can withdraw the permission at any time in Android Settings.

Network access

The app does not request the Internet permission and makes no network connections of its own. The only outbound action is when you tap "View in Google Play," which opens your browser or the Play Store app using Android's standard mechanism.

Files and sharing

When you export a result as a PDF, the file is created in the app's private cache on your device. It is shared with another app only if you choose a target in the system share sheet. We do not upload or have access to these files.

Permissions

The only permission the app requests is approximate location, and only when you choose to use it for prayer times (see above). It does not request precise or background location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, or similar.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
